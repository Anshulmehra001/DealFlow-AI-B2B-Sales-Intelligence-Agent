## 🎨 PNG SLIDE CREATION GUIDE

### Tools:
- **Canva**: https://canva.com (easiest, templates available)
- **PowerPoint**: Built-in, export as PNG
- **Figma**: https://figma.com (professional)

### Design Specifications:
- **Resolution**: 1920x1080 (Full HD)
- **Format**: PNG with transparent background (optional)
- **Font**: 
  - Title: Bold, 48-60pt (e.g., Montserrat Bold)
  - Body: Regular, 24-32pt (e.g., Open Sans)
  - Code: Monospace, 20-24pt (e.g., Courier New)
- **Colors**:
  - Primary: #4285F4 (Google Blue)
  - Secondary: #00ED64 (MongoDB Green)
  - Accent: #EA4335 (Google Red)
  - Background: Gradients or solid dark colors

### Quick Canva Template:
1. Go to Canva.com
2. Search "Tech Presentation"
3. Choose 1920x1080 template
4. Replace text with script content
5. Export each slide as PNG

## 🎬 VIDEO EDITING WORKFLOW

### Using DaVinci Resolve (Free):

1. **Import Assets**:
   - All PNG slides
   - All AI voice files (MP3)
   - Screen recording video
   - Background music

2. **Timeline**:
   - Drag PNG slides in order
   - Align voice narration under each slide
   - Insert screen recording where specified
   - Add background music (low volume, 10-20%)

3. **Transitions**:
   - Between PNGs: 0.5 second fade
   - PNG to video: 1 second cross dissolve
   - Video to PNG: 1 second cross dissolve

4. **Effects**:
   - Zoom in slightly on key points
   - Highlight cursor during demo
   - Add text annotations (optional)

5. **Audio**:
   - Voice: Clear, -3dB
   - Music: Background, -20dB
   - Fade in/out music at start/end

6. **Export**:
   - Format: MP4
   - Resolution: 1920x1080
   - Frame rate: 30fps
   - Codec: H.264
   - Bitrate: 10 Mbps
